# SteamRoot user requirements

What the growers, operators and stakeholders of STR-PRB-001 need, written from their side. Product requirements that meet these needs are in STR-PRS-001 (`03-requirements.md`) and the links are in the generated traceability matrix STR-RTM-001 (`03b-traceability.md`). They are proposals to review with the first co-design partners (one market garden and one nursery near Irving, decided by Amish on 2026-10-02, not yet named). Method: STANDARDS section 22.

> **Safety:** SteamRoot is a fired steam generator. The safety needs UR-005, UR-007 and UR-011 take priority over the performance needs when they conflict.

Table 1. User requirements.

| ID | Category | Title | Need | Priority | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| UR-001 | Performance | Clear weeds and disease to working depth | I need the weed seed bank and soil-borne disease cleared in the top 15 cm of a bed without herbicides or fumigants | High | Small organic farm or market garden; nursery (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-002 | Performance | Treat beds at a useful rate | I need to steam beds fast enough that a working day treats a useful length of bed | High | Small organic farm or market garden (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-003 | Performance | Steady steam from one fire | I need steam to keep coming from one fire without stopping to restart it | Medium | Operator (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-004 | Cost | Run on wood I already have, sparingly | I need it to burn the prunings, hedgerow wood and pallets I already have, and not much of them, with no diesel or propane | High | Small organic farm (PRB, problem and constraints) |
| UR-005 | Safety | Never a pressure vessel | I need a steam generator that cannot build pressure or burst, with little hot water stored in it | High | All users; boiler authority (PRB Table 2) |
| UR-006 | Usability | Run for half a day between refills | I need to steam for a half-day session before refilling water | Medium | Operator (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-007 | Safety | Protect me and the site | I need the fire, sparks, smoke and steam kept away from me, bystanders and dry vegetation | High | Operator; community garden (PRB Tables 1 and 2) |
| UR-008 | Logistics | Tow it with what I have | I need to tow it with a small tractor, utility vehicle or car with a hitch, and move it between farms | High | Farm cooperative; community garden (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-009 | Cost | Afford it, alone or shared | I need it to cost what a small farm or a cooperative can find | Medium | Small farm; farm cooperative (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-010 | Usability | One trained operator, a helper nearby | I need one trained adult to run it, with a second person nearby while steaming | High | Operator (PRB constraints) |
| UR-011 | Regulatory | Legal to build and run | I need the design to clear the boiler authority before anyone builds or fires it | High | Amish; Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (PRB Table 2) |
| UR-012 | Deployment and assembly | Buildable in a farm workshop | I need to build and repair it with stock steel, stock stainless tube and off-the-shelf safety parts | Medium | Open hardware community; farm workshop (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-013 | Environment and durability | Fire outside, steam inside | I need to pipe steam into a polytunnel or greenhouse while the fire stays outside | Medium | Nursery or seedling grower (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-014 | Maintenance | Open and documented | I need an open, documented reference design I can inspect, teach from and study | Low | Extension educator or researcher (PRB Table 1) |

Categories: deployment and assembly, performance, usability, safety, environment and durability, logistics, maintenance, cost, regulatory. Priorities: High, Medium, Low.
