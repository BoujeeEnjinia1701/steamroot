# BOM notes

Prices are indicative concept estimates (TRL 2) and will be confirmed with named suppliers at TRL 3. Item numbers match the callouts in `media/exploded.png`. Items 13 to 15 are not drawn in the massing model.

- Parts total about $1,500, which uses the whole $1,500 concept budget and leaves no contingency. A budget change is proposed in `docs/REVIEW.md`, awaiting Amish; the budget in `project.yaml` is unchanged.
- The largest cost risks are the stainless coil (item 6), the certified relief valve (item 10) and the steam hose (item 11). None of these should be substituted with uncertified or hot-water-rated parts to save money.
- The trailer price assumes a used unit. A new trailer would add about $400 to $700.
- Tools, welding consumables and wood fuel are not included.

> **Safety:** Items 9, 10, 11 and 15 are safety parts. Buy them new, rated for saturated steam, and keep the receipts and ratings with the build records.
